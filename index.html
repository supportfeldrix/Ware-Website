Our Story

Performance Isn't Given.
It's Earned.

At WARE, we craft premium custom teamwear and apparel built to carry your colours, your crest and your pride. From rugby and cricket to netball, hockey and soccer, every piece is designed to reflect the identity of the team that wears it.

We partner with clubs, schools, corporates and event organisers to deliver bespoke kit that performs on the field and stands out off it — jerseys, ceremonial caps, training apparel, team bags and branded gear, all finished to a standard your team can be proud of.

Standout design meets serious quality. With a wide range of styles, fabrics and finishes, we tailor every order to your vision — fusing creativity, comfort and durability into kit that's made to be worn hard and worn with pride.

From personalised sportswear to professional branded apparel, WARE is the trusted partner for custom teamwear that makes a lasting impression.
